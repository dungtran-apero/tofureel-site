TofuReel — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the TofuReel app for Android (package com.tofureel.shortdrama.series, the "App") handles information. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

In short: TofuReel has no sign-up, no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics. Your watch history, saved titles and settings stay on your device.

1. Information we collect

The App does not ask for an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files or precise location. We do not run our own servers that receive your personal data.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Your app language and content language choices.
  • Playback settings, such as Autoplay next episode.
  • Your Library: the Diary of series you have watched, your progress, and titles you have Saved.
  • Recent searches, and posters and video cached for smoother playback.

This data is kept only on your device and is not sent to us.

3. Third-party content provider

Series, episodes, posters and the catalog are delivered by a third-party short drama SDK provided by BytePlus (BytePlus Pte. Ltd.). To stream content and protect it, this SDK may process technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version, network information, IP address and an SDK-generated device identifier. This processing is governed by the BytePlus Privacy Policy: byteplus.com/en/legal/privacy-policy.

4. Advertising, analytics and payments

The current version of the App shows no ads, contains no in-app purchases or subscriptions, and does not include analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. If this changes, we will update this policy before the change takes effect.

5. Permissions

The App only uses Internet and network-state access to stream videos. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or storage.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information. Technical data is processed by the content provider only as described in section 3.

7. Data retention and deletion

Settings → Clear cache removes cached posters and videos. To remove all App data, including your Diary and Saved titles, use Android Settings → Apps → TofuReel → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the App.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

Content is streamed over encrypted connections. Data stored on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox.

10. International users

The content provider may process technical data in countries other than your own, under safeguards described in its privacy policy.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

12. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us through the developer contact details listed on the TofuReel page on Google Play.
